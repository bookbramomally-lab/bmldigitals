UPDATE YOUR NETLIFY PORTFOLIO
From the terminal inside BML-Portfolio, with the Netlify CLI already installed:

netlify link
Choose your existing BML portfolio site, then:
netlify deploy --prod --dir .
Select your portfolio, not another business website.
Alternatively, upload the entire BML-Portfolio folder through Netlify's manual deploy.

THE EXPERIENCE
Your original logo expands smoothly with scrolling and returns to its original size when scrolling back. It does not rotate.
The homepage project theatre moves through four real website previews. Its project buttons jump between them and support arrow keys.
On small or short viewports and with reduced motion enabled, the theatre becomes a normal readable list.
The Work page and each case study have scroll controlled homepage films.
Every preview includes Explore detail. A custom Canvas lens magnifies the actual project image or video frame. Move it with a mouse, drag on a touch screen, or use arrow keys after opening it. Home recentres the lens and Escape closes it.
Magnification and Preview position controls let visitors inspect a frame and scrub the film. Scrolling does not override the film while inspection is open.
With reduced motion, scroll animation stops but visitors can still explicitly inspect and scrub the previews. Without Canvas, the preview position control remains available. Without JavaScript, the original previews and project links remain visible.
Closing the lens releases its Canvas buffer. Rendering responds to input and media events rather than a continuous idle animation.
Titles and previews use matching native page transitions where supported. Ordinary page navigation remains the fallback.
Services use keyboard accessible native disclosure panels. The mobile menu supports keyboard focus and Escape.
The professional profile can be printed or saved as a PDF.
Page transitions use the browser's native view transition feature where supported, with normal navigation elsewhere.
