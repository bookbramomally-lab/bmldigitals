I’m Raheem Ibrahim Opeyemi, a Full Stack Developer, Digital Solution Architect and the founder of BML, based in Lagos, Nigeria. My work brings together design and development across corporate websites, fashion ecommerce, education platforms and independent product concepts.

I enjoy turning ideas into practical digital experiences. From planning the content and interface to building the functionality, I focus on making each website easy to navigate and useful to the people who visit it.

Through BML, I work with businesses and brands to shape their online presence. I stay involved from the first conversation through development, deployment and the improvements that follow.
